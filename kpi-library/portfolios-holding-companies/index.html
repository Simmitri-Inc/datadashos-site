Why KPIs matter

When each business uses its own software and its own definitions, group-level numbers can't be trusted. Shared KPI definitions let you compare companies fairly, spot problems early, and see combined performance in one place.

Example scenario

Example scenario

A holding company owned several businesses, each with its own accounting and CRM. Monthly roll-ups took days and the numbers never quite matched. With shared KPI definitions, each company kept its own dashboard while leadership saw one group scorecard. They spotted an underperforming company early and tracked referrals between businesses for the first time.

How DataDashOS helps

Each company keeps a private dashboard. The group sees an authorized shared view on the same definitions, without replacing anyone's software.
