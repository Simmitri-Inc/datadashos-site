Why KPIs matter

Tech companies can grow fast and burn cash faster. KPIs show whether growth is healthy and sustainable.

The core KPIs

KPIWhat it tells youWhy it matters
Monthly recurring revenuePredictable monthly incomeCore measure of growth
Customer churnCustomers who cancelShows product fit and retention
Customer acquisition costCost to win a customerKeeps growth efficient
Customer lifetime valueRevenue per customer over timeShows long-term value
Net revenue retentionRevenue kept and expanded from existing customersSignals healthy growth
Burn rate and runwayCash spent per month and months leftKeeps the company alive

Example scenario

Example scenario

A SaaS startup was adding customers every month but cash was shrinking. Tracking churn and acquisition cost together showed new customers were leaving before they paid back what it cost to win them. They improved onboarding and watched churn fall.
