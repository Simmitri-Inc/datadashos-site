Why KPIs matter

Practices balance patient care, scheduling and billing. KPIs show where appointments, time and revenue slip away.

The core KPIs

KPIWhat it tells youWhy it matters
Appointment utilisationBooked slots out of available slotsShows capacity use
No-show rateMissed appointmentsLost revenue and care gaps
Days in accounts receivableTime to get paidProtects cash flow
Claim denial rateClaims rejected first timeExposes billing problems
New patient growthNew patients per monthMeasures practice growth
Patient satisfactionSurvey scores and reviewsDrives retention and referrals

Operational KPIs most practices miss

KPIWhat it tells youWhy it matters
Third next available appointmentDays until the third open slotThe standard measure of real access
Provider productivityVisits or value units per provider per dayShows capacity and fairness
Check-in-to-checkout timeTotal patient time in the clinicFlow and satisfaction
Late cancellation rateCancellations within 24 hoursSlots that can't be refilled
Clean claim rateClaims accepted with no correctionsFaster payment, less admin
Collection ratePayments collected vs. allowed amountMoney actually received
Patient recall completionDue follow-ups actually bookedCare continuity and revenue
Referral leakageReferrals sent outside your networkLost revenue and continuity

Example scenario

Example scenario

A multi-provider clinic struggled with cash flow despite a busy schedule. Tracking no-shows and claim denials showed both were higher than the team realised. They added reminders, fixed common billing errors, and reviewed both numbers every week.
