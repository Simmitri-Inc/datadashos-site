Why KPIs matter

Small losses in uptime, scrap and delivery add up fast. KPIs make them visible before they eat your margin.

The core KPIs

KPIWhat it tells youWhy it matters
Overall equipment effectivenessAvailability × performance × qualityThe core measure of production efficiency
On-time deliveryOrders shipped on scheduleProtects customer trust
Scrap and rework rateOutput that fails qualityDirect cost and waste
Unplanned downtimeHours lost to breakdownsLost capacity
Cost per unitTotal cost to make one unitDrives pricing and margin
Inventory turnoverHow fast materials and goods moveFrees up cash

Example scenario

Example scenario

A mid-sized manufacturer kept missing delivery dates. Tracking downtime and on-time delivery side by side showed one machine caused most of the delays. A maintenance schedule and a weekly KPI review got deliveries back on track.
