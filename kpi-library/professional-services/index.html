Why KPIs matter

In a services firm, time is the product. KPIs show where hours go, which clients are profitable, and when to hire.

The core KPIs

KPIWhat it tells youWhy it matters
Billable utilisationShare of hours billed to clientsCore driver of profit
Realisation rateBilled value actually collectedExposes write-offs and discounts
Revenue per employeeOutput per personShows efficiency as you grow
Client profitabilityMargin by clientShows who to grow or reprice
Pipeline valueProposals in progressPredicts future workload
Client retentionClients who renew or returnMeasures relationship strength

Example scenario

Example scenario

A 15-person consulting firm felt overworked but under-earning. Tracking utilisation and client profitability showed two large clients consumed far more hours than they paid for. The firm renegotiated scope and freed capacity for better-paying work.
