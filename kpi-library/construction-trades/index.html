Why KPIs matter

In construction, margin is won or lost on the job site. Without clear KPIs, you find out a job lost money weeks after it's finished.

The core KPIs

KPIWhat it tells youWhy it matters
Gross margin per jobProfit after direct costsShows which jobs actually make money
Estimate vs. actual costHow accurate your bids areStops underpriced work
Close rateBids won out of bids sentShows sales effectiveness
BacklogSigned work not yet startedPredicts cash flow and staffing
Days to collectTime from invoice to paymentProtects cash flow
Safety incidentsIncidents per hours workedProtects people and insurance costs

Example scenario

Example scenario

A 25-person contractor was booked solid but cash was always tight. After defining KPIs, they saw that one job type ran far over estimate every time. They repriced it, tightened change orders, and tracked collection time weekly. Within a quarter, margin and cash flow were both measurable and improving.
