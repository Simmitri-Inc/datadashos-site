Why KPIs matter

Retail margins are thin. Inventory, conversion and customer value decide whether growth makes you money or just keeps you busy.

The core KPIs

KPIWhat it tells youWhy it matters
Conversion rateVisitors who buyShows store and site effectiveness
Average order valueRevenue per orderA key growth lever
Gross marginProfit after product costsShows real profitability
Inventory turnoverHow fast stock sellsFrees up cash
Customer acquisition costCost to win a customerKeeps marketing profitable
Repeat purchase rateCustomers who buy againBuilds lifetime value

Example scenario

Example scenario

An online store was growing revenue but cash kept running short. Tracking inventory turnover showed slow-moving stock tying up cash. They cut slow lines, bundled products to lift order value, and reviewed both KPIs weekly.
