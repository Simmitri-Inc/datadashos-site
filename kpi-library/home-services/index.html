Why KPIs matter

Home services run on speed and follow-up. A few points of booking rate or average ticket can be the difference between a good year and a great one.

The core KPIs

KPIWhat it tells youWhy it matters
Lead-to-booked rateCalls and leads that become appointmentsShows how well you convert demand
Speed to leadTime to first responseFaster responses win more jobs
Average ticketRevenue per jobShows pricing and upsell strength
Cost per lead by channelMarketing spend per leadShows where to spend
Repeat and membership rateCustomers who come backBuilds steady revenue
Callback rateJobs needing a return visitMeasures quality

Example scenario

Example scenario

An HVAC company spent heavily on ads but couldn't say which ones worked. Tracking cost per lead and booking rate by channel showed one channel delivered most of the booked jobs. They shifted budget there and set a target response time for every lead.
