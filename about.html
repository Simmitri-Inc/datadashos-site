Company

Built inside the business it was meant to run

DataDashOS is not a generic analytics wrapper. It is the operating rhythm Simmitri needed — now packaged for other owner-led companies.

Key takeaways

Simmitri, Inc. has operated solar and roofing in Northern California for 32 years. DataDashOS was built to give those operators one KPI system, a weekly review, and AI-guided next steps — then productized for other SMBs.

Why we built DataDashOS

Owner-led businesses already pay for accounting software, a CRM, and ads platforms. What they still lack is a single place where those numbers become a weekly decision. Spreadsheets fill the gap until they break under growth.

We felt that pain running Simmitri. So we built the loop we needed: connect tools, measure the right KPIs, review every week with AI, and assign the actions that follow.
