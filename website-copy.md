Simmitri Holdings brings two operating companies and 32 years of contractor know-how. DataDashOS unifies performance across those stacks into shared KPIs, weekly AI reviews, and owned actions — without replacing existing software.

### Why we built DataDashOS
Contractor businesses already pay for accounting software, a CRM, and ads platforms. What they still lack is a shared scorecard where those numbers become a weekly decision. Spreadsheets fill the gap until they break under growth.

We felt that pain running Simmitri Holdings. So we built the loop we needed: define KPIs, connect data, see performance, review every week with AI, and assign the actions that follow.
