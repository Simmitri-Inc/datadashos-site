Material shortages: KPIs shared with your supplier
Every hot-shot delivery costs money and crew time. Track these with your supply house to cut shortages at the source:
• Short-shipped job rate: jobs delivered missing material
• Shortages by material type and cause: takeoff error, out of stock, picking error or field change
• Hot-shot deliveries and cost: count and dollars per month, by crew
• Crew wait time from shortages: hours lost waiting
• Shortage trend: proof that fixes are working
• Shortage fill cost and time by delivery method: hot-shot vs. courier vs. freight, cost per delivery and hours to site
