FAQ

What is DataDashOS? ▾
DataDashOS is a KPI-first operating system for growing businesses. Instead of starting with dashboards, it starts by deciding which numbers define success for your business, then builds a weekly rhythm of review and action around them.
Who is it for? ▾
Owner-led and growing businesses that want one shared scorecard and a weekly rhythm for improving it.
Does it replace my existing software? ▾
No. It sits on top of your accounting, CRM and marketing tools.
How is it different from a BI dashboard? ▾
BI shows data. DataDashOS starts by defining what to measure, then turns the numbers into weekly decisions and owned actions.
How does the AI coach use my data? ▾
Its recommendations are grounded in your own KPIs and reviews, so advice stays tied to measurable results.
