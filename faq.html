Frequently asked questions

Direct answers written for humans, featured snippets, and AI citation — one clear passage per question.

What is DataDashOS? ▾
DataDashOS is the KPI intelligence and AI orchestration layer for contractor businesses. It unifies performance across existing tools into shared KPIs, weekly AI reviews, and owned actions.
Who should use DataDashOS? ▾
Contractor owners and contractor portfolios that already run accounting, CRM, and marketing tools but need one shared scorecard and weekly operating rhythm.
What integrations are supported? ▾
QuickBooks Online, GoHighLevel, and Google Ads are first-class connections, with CSV import — sitting on top of existing systems rather than replacing them.
Does DataDashOS replace my BI tool? ▾
It is built for owners, not analysts. The differentiator is contractor KPI blueprints, weekly AI review to owned actions, and a shared KPI registry across a group — not only charts.
Is DataDashOS available to buy today? ▾
Early access demos are open now. General self-serve pricing will follow later releases.
How does the AI coach use my data? ▾
The coach is grounded in your connected KPIs and weekly reviews so recommendations stay tied to measurable business changes, with workspace controls and audit logs for teams.
