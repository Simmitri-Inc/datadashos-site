The enterprise problem

More AI. Less control.

AI tools are multiplying across every department. But most send your data to someone else's servers, work outside your definitions of success, and leave no clear record of what they did. For regulated or privacy-conscious companies, that's a deal-breaker.

How It Works

Early access

Your AI, on your servers

Your own AI agents connect to your CRM, ERP, finance and operations systems inside your environment. Raw data never has to leave it.

Outside AI, without sharing your data

Want the power of leading AI models? DataDashOS sends them only structured KPI summaries, never raw records, customer details or account data.

One KPI layer over everything

Every agent, internal or external, reports into the same KPI definitions, owners and targets. So all your AI is measured against what actually matters.

Governance built in

Role-based access, human approval for key decisions, escalation paths and permanent audit logs. You always know what your AI did and why.

Security FAQ

Where does my data live? ▾
On your own servers or private cloud. DataDashOS's KPI layer only sees structured KPI data, never your raw records.
Who can see it? ▾
Only the people you authorise. Role-based access controls who sees which KPIs, and every action is logged.
Can we use our own AI model? ▾
Yes. Connect AI models running on your own servers, outside models, or both.
Do outside AI providers see our data? ▾
Only the KPI summaries you choose to send. No customer details, financials or raw files.
What happens if we leave? ▾
Your data never left your environment, so there's nothing to extract. Your KPI definitions and history can be exported.
