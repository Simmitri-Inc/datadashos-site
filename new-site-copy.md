# KPI Library: Construction & Trades

**Headline:** KPIs for construction and trades businesses.

**Pain point:** Busy crews and full schedules don't always mean profitable jobs.

**Why KPIs matter:** In construction, margin is won or lost on the job site. Without clear KPIs, you find out a job lost money weeks after it's finished.

| KPI | What it tells you | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gross margin per job | Profit after direct costs | Shows which jobs actually make money |
| Estimate vs. actual cost | How accurate your bids are | Stops underpriced work |
| Close rate | Bids won out of bids sent | Shows sales effectiveness |
| Backlog | Signed work not yet started | Predicts cash flow and staffing |
| Days to collect | Time from invoice to payment | Protects cash flow |
| Safety incidents | Incidents per hours worked | Protects people and insurance costs |

**Example scenario:** A 25-person contractor was booked solid but cash was always tight. After defining KPIs, they saw that one job type ran far over estimate every time. They repriced it, tightened change orders, and tracked collection time weekly. Within a quarter, margin and cash flow were both measurable and improving.
